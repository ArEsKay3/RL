## 3. Every place the two inference engines differ on the Mamba path

None of these is a dtype/precision asymmetry; the fork's SSD, state-passing, chunk-scan, conv and decode kernels are literal ports of vLLM's ("Adapted from vLLM project" headers, `ops/ssd_combined.py:4`, `ops/ssd_state_passing.py:4`, `ops/ssd_chunk_state.py:4`, `ops/mamba_ssm.py:5`).

| # | Difference | MINF | vLLM | Precision relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | prefix reuse across agent turns | off: every turn re-prefills the whole history through the chunked SSD path (`minf.yaml:32`) | on, `mamba_cache_mode=align` ("experimental" per the 0.25.1 log): fp32 SSM + bf16 conv states cached at block-aligned boundaries and reused (`mixer2.py:640-700, 760-812`; `cache.py:132-139`) | topology, not dtype: the state at the start of a turn comes from a cached earlier prefill (vLLM) vs a fresh full prefill (MINF). | D2 | prefill chunk grid | engine steps of <= 16384 tokens, block 256 (`minf.yaml:29-31`); chunk metadata precomputed (`dynamic_context.py:1002-1021, 1219`) | steps of <= 8480 tokens (not a multiple of 128) with logical chunks re-aligned per sequence (`mamba2_attn.py:21-87`) | rounding order only; the carried state crosses the cache in fp32 in both |

## 7. Ranked candidate mechanisms

1. **T1 - bf16 inter-chunk SSM states in the trainer vs fp32 in both engines.** The only material dtype asymmetry on the Mamba path, and it scales with sequence length. It is common to both arms, so on its own it predicts equal long-context trainer/engine mismatch in both arms (consistent with the equal `lp_error` observed in steps 1-10); it can contribute to the MINF/vLLM gap only through an interaction with an engine-specific difference in *which* long sequences get generated and kept. Tests: E1, E2, E7.
2. **D1 - prefix-cache topology (vLLM on + align, MINF off).** Not a dtype difference; state provenance per turn differs. Align mode is flagged experimental by vLLM 0.25.1. Chain I (MINF with prefix caching) and chain D (vLLM serving MINF weights) reportedly did not change the picture, so this is a low-probability mechanism; test E4.
3. **D2/D3 - prefill chunk grid and decode-kernel backend.** Rounding-order-level differences; lowest prior. Tests E5, E6.

Explicitly *not* found on the Mamba path: any bf16 softplus/exp/cumsum, any bf16 SSM state in either engine, a stale decode `A` cache, FP8 or TF32 on Mamba tensors, an eps or group-size mismatch, or any dtype in which MINF differs from vLLM.
