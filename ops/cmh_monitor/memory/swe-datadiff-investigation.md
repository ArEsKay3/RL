**Why:** rkirby's question was to find the data difference; the answer shapes the fix (training signal, not engine).

**How to apply:** for new arms compute E(long)/E(near-rep) from token dumps at step 10 as an early warning; proposed fixes = repetition-aware penalty (include zlib 0.10-0.25 band, std floor with LOO norm) or masked replays (A: G data minus long-think rollouts; B: M data minus long-think failures).
